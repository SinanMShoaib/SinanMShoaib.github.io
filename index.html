Shorts Uploader

A personal tool that publishes short videos to its owner’s own YouTube channel.

What it is

Shorts Uploader is a small private application built and used by one person to publish videos to a single YouTube channel that they own. It is not a product, not a service, and is not offered to other people. There is no sign-up, no hosted website to log into, and no way for anyone else to use it.

What it does

  • Prepares short videos on the owner’s own computer.
  • Signs in to the owner’s own YouTube account, with their permission, through Google’s standard consent screen.
  • Uploads those videos to that account as private drafts.
  • Reads back basic details of its own uploads to confirm they arrived.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect information from anyone other than its owner.
  • It does not sell, share, or transmit data to third parties.
  • It does not display advertising or run analytics.
  • It has no other users, and no accounts can be created.

This page exists because Google requires a public homepage, privacy policy, and terms of service for any application that uses OAuth to access a Google account — including a private one used only by its author.
